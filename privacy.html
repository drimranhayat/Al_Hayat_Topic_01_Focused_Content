Privacy and your study data

Al-Hayat Hadith is prepared by Al-Hayat Centre for Islamic Studies. This policy describes the bundled app in this repository.

Your information stays on your device

The app has no account system, advertisements, analytics or developer-operated data collection service. Bookmarks, notes, reading progress, quiz answers and reading settings are stored locally. We do not receive them.

Backups and sharing

If you export a study backup, you choose where to keep it. If you share text or a backup through another app, that app handles it under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local study data. Back up important notes first.

External source links

Lessons work offline. Opening a classical-source or translation link uses the internet and opens an external website. Those services may receive ordinary connection information and have their own privacy policies. The website version is served by its hosting provider, which may process access logs.

Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera access. Native sharing is initiated by you.

Contact and release status

The publisher must add its verified support email and final policy date before public release. This policy is a release draft until those details are supplied.
